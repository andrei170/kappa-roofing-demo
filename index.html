What we do

Roof replacement

Full tear-off and replacement on flat and low-slope commercial roofs, planned around your tenants and your trading hours rather than ours.

  • Survey and written specification first
  • Phased where the building cannot close
  • Our own crew
Repairs & leak diagnosis

Finding where the water is actually getting in, which is rarely where it appears inside, and fixing that rather than replacing a roof that has years left in it.

  • Diagnosis before quoting
  • We will tell you if it does not need replacing
  • Emergency response
